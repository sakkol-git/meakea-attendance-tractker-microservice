# Start here

## Ten-minute orientation

Read the repository baseline, then the dashboard. This is a build-along plan, not finished software. Every implementation step is **Not run** until you execute it and record evidence. Commands referencing proposed files require their creation in the named earlier step.

## The five daily files

1. [You are here](YOU-ARE-HERE.md): the next action and last green tag.
1. [Current build guide](01-FOUNDATION/BUILD-GUIDE.md): one small step.
1. [Current gate](01-FOUNDATION/VERIFY-AND-GATE.md): observable evidence.
1. [Stuck protocol](STUCK-PROTOCOL.md): bounded recovery.
1. [Global checklist](GLOBAL-CHECKLIST.md): requirement coverage.

## Daily five-minute routine

- Read tomorrow's saved action, choose one step, and check its prerequisite.
- Spend no more than two minutes checking energy and blockers before starting a timer.
- Work one 30 to 90 minute step; maximum two new concepts per day.
- Run its check, commit only intended files, and tag only at a proven stone boundary.
- Finish by writing the exact next action in the dashboard, recording evidence, and closing the laptop.

## Pace and boundaries

Three build days, Thursday buffer without new scope, Friday gate-and-deploy, two rest days. Hours interpret the user reply as 24 each, 70 percent planned; weeks 4 and 8 have 15 percent less planned time. Never pay for missed work with weekends or skipped security/restore tests. If a critical gate fails, carry it into the next week's first stone and cut stretch work.
