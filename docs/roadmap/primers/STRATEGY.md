# Strategy candidate

## Read in ten to twenty minutes

What: A strategy replaces an interchangeable algorithm behind one interface.

Why HRFlow needs this: Attendance could eventually have multiple approved calculation policies.

## Tiny example

A clear late/early calculation function is the current default.

## Common mistake

> **Pitfall:** Introducing strategies during correctness work can hide precedence bugs.

## Try and explain

- [ ] Two real policy variations must exist before refactor.
- [ ] Explain the concept in two sentences before returning to the build step.
